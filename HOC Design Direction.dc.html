01

What changed from v1

White is the canvas

Pure white pages, light grey panels for grouping, near-black type. The obsidian room survives only where it earns its keep: the owner cockpit and the fleet map. Contrast comes from photography, not from dark chrome.

One grotesque, no serif

A single neo-grotesque family carries display, UI and data. Hierarchy is made with weight, size and negative tracking — never with a second typeface. Display sits at 500, body at 400, captions at 400 in grey.

Pills, not squares

Controls are fully rounded: solid black primary, light grey secondary, grey chips for tags and status. Containers stay square with hairlines. No shadows, no gradients, no borders on imagery.

06

Rules & motion

Motion
page enter — 500ms fade + 12px rise, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)
overlay — 380ms wipe, rows stagger 40ms
hover — 160ms ease-out, fill and underline only, never scale
numerals — 900ms count-up, once, tabular figures
totals — 240ms tween on change only
reduced motion — opacity only, counters render final value
Non-negotiables
No shadows, no gradients except the logo leaf. Depth is hairlines and grey panels.
Radius: 999px controls and chips, 8px cards, 4px fields, 0 containers.
One gold element per screen. If gold reads as decoration, delete it.
Imagery is full-bleed or centred on a grey plate. Never rounded, never bordered, never a car on a photographic background in UI.
No uppercase tracking, no serif, no second typeface, no icon set beyond account, chevron, close and map pin.
Body never below 15px; spec numerals never below 56px desktop; tap targets 44px minimum.
